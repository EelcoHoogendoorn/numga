# Articles

These articles explore the conceptual consequences of geometric algebra and extensors, the multilinear maps between multivector spaces, across geometry, calculus and physics. All assume basic geometric algebra, and the list follows a suggested reading order. Runnable simulations paired with their code live in a [video gallery at numga.org](https://numga.org/), in [illustrated extensor examples](../docs/extensors.md) and across [example notebooks](../examples/README.md).

1. [Extensors through multiple lenses](multiple_lenses.md): extensors viewed as partial applications of products, as geometric objects that transform with their parts, and as multilinear maps.
2. [Why orientation](orientation.md): intrinsic and extrinsic orientation in geometric algebra, motivated by what a mirror does to geometric constructions and to physical observables such as kinetic energy.
3. [The regressive product](regressive_product.md): the regressive product from its metric-free volume definition across orientations to geometric joins, partial contractions, physical observables and linear maps (assumes [1](multiple_lenses.md), [2](orientation.md)).
4. [Complementarity](complementarity.md): one metric-free regressive pairing for quantities that move with a space and quantities that measure or act on them, across dimensions, mirrors and linear maps (assumes [1](multiple_lenses.md), [2](orientation.md), [3](regressive_product.md)).
5. [Extensors, linear algebra, tensors and forms](frameworks.md): how linear algebra, tensors, exterior forms and specialized algebras sit inside geometric algebra and extensors (assumes [1](multiple_lenses.md), [4](complementarity.md)).
6. [Extensors and common practice](common_practice.md): worked applied problems in estimation, robotics, graphics, mechanics and electromagnetism compared with their extensor counterparts (assumes [4](complementarity.md), [5](frameworks.md)).
7. [Discrete differential geometric algebra](ddga.md): calculus and physics on a simplicial topology from the simplex gradient, with values of every grade and operators of any arity (assumes [1](multiple_lenses.md), [3](regressive_product.md), [4](complementarity.md)).
8. [Geometry and quantities](thermodynamics.md): spacetime and projective geometry joined with degenerate thermodynamic directions, relating Maxwell's and Onsager's relations through a material map and its adjugate (assumes [4](complementarity.md), [7](ddga.md)).
