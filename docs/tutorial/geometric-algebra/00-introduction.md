# 0. Introduction

This tutorial is for readers who know vectors and the scalar product and want a short introduction to geometric algebra: what it is, and why it is worth learning. Complex numbers and quaternions help but are not needed.

Geometric algebra multiplies vectors with a single product, the geometric product, and finds turns, reflections, areas, volumes and intersections all inside that one product. Complex numbers and quaternions turn up inside it too, as parts of the algebra of ordinary space.

The tutorial starts from one small change to the multiplication rules of the scalar product and follows its consequences one short chapter at a time, in plain three-dimensional space. Each chapter states its idea in mathematics, then shows it in small pieces of numga code, each followed by the value it computes, and ends with links to runnable examples that use it.

It is a primer, not a treatise: an entry point that sketches each idea and leaves the full treatment to the literature. The [closing chapter](17-further-reading.md) points onwards, to the applications, the topics left out and further reading. The companion [extensor tutorial](../extensors/00-introduction.md) builds on this one.
