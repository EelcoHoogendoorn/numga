# 8. The multiplication table of three-dimensional space

The products of the eight basis elements of three-dimensional space, $1$, $x$, $y$, $z$, $xy$, $xz$, $yz$ and $xyz$, viewed as a multiplication table:

|         | $1$     | $x$     | $y$     | $z$     | $xy$    | $xz$    | $yz$    | $xyz$   |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| $1$     | $1$     | $x$     | $y$     | $z$     | $xy$    | $xz$    | $yz$    | $xyz$   |
| $x$     | $x$     | $1$     | $xy$    | $xz$    | $y$     | $z$     | $xyz$   | $yz$    |
| $y$     | $y$     | $-xy$   | $1$     | $yz$    | $-x$    | $-xyz$  | $z$     | $-xz$   |
| $z$     | $z$     | $-xz$   | $-yz$   | $1$     | $xyz$   | $-x$    | $-y$    | $xy$    |
| $xy$    | $xy$    | $-y$    | $x$     | $xyz$   | $-1$    | $-yz$   | $xz$    | $-z$    |
| $xz$    | $xz$    | $-z$    | $-xyz$  | $x$     | $yz$    | $-1$    | $-xy$   | $y$     |
| $yz$    | $yz$    | $xyz$   | $-z$    | $y$     | $-xz$   | $xy$    | $-1$    | $-x$    |
| $xyz$   | $xyz$   | $yz$    | $-xz$   | $xy$    | $-z$    | $y$     | $-x$    | $-1$    |

Every entry is one of the eight, up to sign. The products of basis vectors never lead to anything new: the eight are closed under the geometric product, and form the whole algebra of three-dimensional space. Each is a basis element of that algebra, and every element of it is a sum of the eight with coefficients.
